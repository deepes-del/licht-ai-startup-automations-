JOBSner Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to JOBSner.

JOBSner is a job search and recruitment platform that helps candidates discover job opportunities, create profiles, apply for jobs, save jobs, and communicate with recruiters.

This Privacy Policy explains how JOBSner collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the JOBSner mobile application and related services.

By using JOBSner, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Password/authentication information
  • Profile photo
  • Account and authentication identifiers

JOBSner may use authentication services such as Firebase Authentication to securely authenticate users.

2.2 Profile Information

Depending on the information you choose to provide, your profile may contain:

  • Name
  • Contact information
  • Location
  • Education
  • Work experience
  • Skills
  • Job preferences
  • Resume or other employment-related information
  • Profile photo
  • Other information you voluntarily provide

2.3 Job Applications

When you apply for a job through JOBSner, we collect information associated with your application, including:

  • Candidate information
  • Job applied for
  • Application date
  • Application status
  • Application history
  • Information provided as part of your application

This information may be made available to the recruiter or employer responsible for the relevant job.

2.4 Documents

If you choose to upload employment-related documents, such as a resume or other supporting documents, JOBSner may collect and store those documents to provide recruitment services.

You should only upload documents that you are comfortable sharing for employment purposes.

2.5 Device and Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the application, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Application version
  • Authentication and security information
  • Error and diagnostic information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate users
  • Display your candidate profile
  • Show relevant job opportunities
  • Process job applications
  • Allow recruiters to review applications
  • Allow recruiters to contact candidates regarding relevant job opportunities
  • Save jobs and application information
  • Send application and job-related notifications
  • Maintain application history
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Maintain the security of JOBSner
  • Improve and maintain our services
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

4. Sharing of Information

JOBSner does not sell your personal information for money.

Information may be shared with the following categories of parties when necessary to provide the service:

Recruiters and Employers

When you apply for a job, information associated with your application and candidate profile may be made available to the recruiter or employer responsible for that job.

Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers that help us operate JOBSner, including authentication, database, hosting, security, analytics, and infrastructure services.

These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law
  • Respond to lawful requests
  • Protect the rights and safety of users
  • Prevent fraud or abuse
  • Protect the security of JOBSner

5. Firebase Authentication

JOBSner may use Firebase Authentication to provide secure account authentication, including supported authentication methods such as phone, email/password, and Google Sign-In.

Authentication information is processed according to the applicable Firebase services and Google's privacy practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials and authentication information secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide JOBSner services, maintain account and application records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it in accordance with applicable requirements.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your JOBSner account and associated personal information.

To request account deletion, use the account deletion option provided within the application, where available, or contact us using the contact information provided below.

When a valid deletion request is processed, we will delete or anonymize applicable personal information, subject to information that we are legally required or reasonably permitted to retain.

Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

JOBSner is intended for users seeking employment opportunities and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to JOBSner, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Third-Party Services

JOBSner may use third-party services necessary for authentication, hosting, database management, security, and application functionality.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the Last Updated date shown at the beginning of this policy.

Users are encouraged to periodically review this Privacy Policy.
